## Email / LinkedIn message (short version)

> Subject: a context-adjusted IPL valuation model — calibrated, and honest about where it isn't
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I built a small project on the problem your team works on: raw T20 stats are
> context-blind and small-sample-noisy, so every number a coach actually wants
> sits exactly where those two problems bite. I rebuilt the conceptual core of a
> Smart-Stats / WinViz-style engine from public cricsheet data — an
> expected-runs model, a calibrated ball-by-ball win-probability model, and
> partially-pooled player effects — and focused on the modelling judgement
> rather than the data: walk-forward validation, phase-wise calibration, and
> shrinkage of small-sample effects.
>
> A 60-second path through the app: open a recent final, watch the
> win-probability ribbon and the projected-score fan, then open the **Model
> card** tab — it publishes where the models are calibrated and where a public
> dataset simply can't compete.
